FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to know my level before starting?

Not at all. Your free trial includes a short level assessment, and we'll place you in the right class based on the results.

Are classes online, in person, or both?

Both! You can join live online classes from anywhere, visit our campus, or mix the two — whatever suits your schedule.

What ages do you teach?

Everyone from age 5 to adult learners. We have dedicated programs for kids, teens, adults, and exam preparation.

Can I cancel or pause my plan?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract. You can pause or cancel anytime from your account.

How big are the classes?

Group classes are capped at 8 students so everyone gets plenty of speaking time. Private plans are fully 1:1.
